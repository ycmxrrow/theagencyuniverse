Cookie Policy

Effective date / last updated: November 25, 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They are commonly used to keep you signed in, remember preferences, measure traffic (analytics cookies), and deliver advertising (marketing cookies). This policy explains how The Agency Universe, based in Grand Rapids, Michigan, uses — and does not use — cookies, and it forms part of our Privacy Policy.

2. We set no cookies

These websites are static pages. We do not set any cookies — no strictly-necessary session cookies (there are no accounts or carts on these pages), no analytics cookies, no functional preference cookies, and no advertising or tracking cookies. We run no analytics software, advertising pixels, or social-media trackers. Because we use no non-essential cookies, no cookie-consent banner is required on these websites, and none is shown.

3. Your browser's cache

Your browser may store (cache) copies of page files so the site loads faster on repeat visits. Caching happens entirely inside your own browser, stores no identifier with us, and can be cleared at any time in your browser settings.

4. Third-party websites

Our storefronts link out to third-party platforms — including Payhip, Stripe, Fiverr, Zazzle, Spreadshirt, YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram — for checkout, media, and merchandise. Those platforms do use cookies under their own cookie and privacy policies, which govern once you are on their sites. We have no control over third-party cookies. You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings; blocking cookies on those platforms may affect their checkout or playback features.

5. Do Not Track

These websites do not track visitors, so they respond the same way to every visitor regardless of "Do Not Track" browser signals.

6. If this ever changes

If we ever add analytics or any other non-essential cookies, we will update this page first, describe the cookies and their purposes here, and obtain your consent before any non-essential cookie is set, as required by applicable law (including the EU ePrivacy Directive and UK PECR where they apply).
