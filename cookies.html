Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The short version

These websites set no cookies. None. We run no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no social-media pixels on them.

What that means

Because we set no cookies, there is nothing here to consent to and no cookie banner to click. Your browser may cache page files so the site loads faster on your next visit — that happens inside your own browser and does not report anything to us.

Other sites you may visit from here

Our storefronts link out to platforms such as Payhip, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Fiverr, Zazzle, and Spreadshirt. Those sites do use cookies under their own policies, which apply once you are on them.

If this ever changes

If we add analytics or any other non-essential cookie in the future, we will update this page first and ask for your consent before it runs.
