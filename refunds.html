Refund Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Digital downloads — Stockpile Media and Signal Labs

Digital products are delivered instantly. If a file is defective, corrupted, or materially different from its description, email us within 14 days of purchase and choose a replacement or a full refund. Duplicate purchases are always refunded. Because a delivered file can be kept and used, change-of-mind purchases are not refundable.

Website services — WebFix Local

If you cancel a website build before work begins, anything you paid is refunded in full. Once your site has been delivered, the build fee is earned. Care plans and Google Business Profile management are month-to-month: you can cancel anytime, cancellation stops the next billing, and the current month is not refunded once that month's work has been done.

Merchandise — Merch Lab

Printed items are made and shipped by Zazzle and Spreadshirt, and their return policies cover those orders. If something arrives wrong, contact us as well and we will help you get it sorted.

Fiverr orders

Orders placed through Fiverr are covered by Fiverr's own resolution process.

How to ask

Email collin@theagencyuniverse.com with the email address you ordered with and the date of purchase. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 10 business days.
