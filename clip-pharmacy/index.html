Clip Pharmacy

Short-form clips from licensed live-music campaigns, on YouTube. Clip Pharmacy is the clip studio inside The Agency Universe — every clip comes from a licensed campaign, and the licensing is the point.

The internet is full of clips nobody had the rights to post. Clip Pharmacy works the other way: artists and campaigns license their live music for short-form use, and the studio cuts the strongest moments into clips built for YouTube and social feeds. Licensed, labeled, and posted in the open.
