WebFix Local

One-page websites for local businesses — built, hosted, and looked after. WebFix Local is the local-services door of The Agency Universe, working out of Grand Rapids for service businesses across Kent County.

Most local shops don't need a bigger website. They need the page a customer lands on after Googling them to load fast, say exactly what the business does, and put a call button where a thumb expects it. That's the whole product — priced flat, in writing, with the finished files handed over at launch.
