ABOUT

A small operation, run like a universe

The Agency Universe is an independent studio operation based in Grand Rapids, Michigan. Each door above is its own store or service, run by a small crew under one standard: working things, honestly labeled, no half measures.

Behind the scenes, an audio plug-in lab and an artist-services guide are in development — they will get their own doors when they are ready, not before.
