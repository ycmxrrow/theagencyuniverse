Privacy Policy

Effective date / last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Who we are

The Agency Universe ("Company," "we," "us") is a digital studio based in Grand Rapids, Kent County, Michigan, United States. We operate the storefronts Clip Pharmacy, Stockpile Media, Merch Lab, and Signal Labs, and the local-services division WebFix Local. For any privacy question or request, contact us at collin@theagencyuniverse.com or (616) 349-8793. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

2. What these websites collect

These websites are static pages. They offer no user accounts, run no analytics or advertising trackers, and set no cookies. Merely viewing a page does not transmit your personal information to us, beyond the standard technical data your browser sends to any web host (such as IP address and browser type), which our hosting provider processes to deliver the page and which we do not use to identify or profile you.

3. Information you send us

If you email, call, or message us, we receive the contact details and content you choose to send. We process it only to respond to you, provide the products or services you requested, and maintain normal business, tax, and accounting records. Our legal bases for this processing, where applicable, are performance of a contract with you, our legitimate business interests, and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Orders, payments, and service providers

Checkouts are processed by third-party platforms acting as our service providers / processors: Payhip and Stripe (digital products and services), Fiverr (gig orders), and Zazzle and Spreadshirt (printed merchandise). Clip Pharmacy campaigns run through Content Rewards and Vyro. Each platform processes your payment and delivery information under its own privacy policy. We receive only the order information needed to deliver and support your purchase (such as your name, email address, and items ordered). We never receive or store your full payment card number.

5. No sale of personal information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose personal information only to the service providers listed above, as needed to operate, or when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect our rights and safety.

6. Retention and security

We retain emails and order records only as long as reasonably necessary for support, tax, and accounting purposes, after which they are deleted. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the small scale of our operation; no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (including under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation where it applies), you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to access or correct it, to request its deletion, to restrict or object to its processing, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To exercise any of these rights, email collin@theagencyuniverse.com. We will verify your request using the email address you contacted us from and respond within 30 days, or within the period your applicable law requires. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Do Not Track

Because these websites run no trackers, they do not respond differently to "Do Not Track" browser signals — there is no tracking to disable.

9. Children

Our products and services are intended for adults and businesses. They are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes and governing terms

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of our websites or services after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. Disputes arising under this policy are governed by the dispute-resolution and governing-law provisions of our Terms of Service (Michigan law; Kent County, Michigan).
