Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Who we are

The Agency Universe is a digital studio based in Grand Rapids, Michigan, USA. We run four storefronts — Clip Pharmacy, Stockpile Media, Merch Lab, and Signal Labs — plus WebFix Local, our local-services division. Questions about this policy: email collin@theagencyuniverse.com or call (616) 349-8793.

What these websites collect

These websites are static pages. They have no user accounts, run no analytics, show no advertising trackers, and set no cookies. Simply viewing a page does not give us your personal information.

What you send us

If you email, call, or message us, we receive the contact details and message content you choose to send. We use it only to reply, do the work you asked about, and keep normal business records. We never sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers below, and only as needed to deliver what you bought.

Orders and payments

Checkouts run through third-party platforms — Payhip and Stripe for our digital products and services, Fiverr for gig orders, and Zazzle and Spreadshirt for printed merchandise. Those platforms process your payment and delivery details under their own privacy policies. We receive the order information needed to deliver and support your purchase (such as your name, email, and what you ordered). We never see or store your full card number.

Clip campaigns

Clip Pharmacy campaigns run through Content Rewards and Vyro. Creators who take part share their information with those platforms under the platforms' own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep emails and order records for as long as we need them for support, taxes, and accounting, then delete them. You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — email collin@theagencyuniverse.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Our products and services are for adults and businesses. They are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date at the top.
